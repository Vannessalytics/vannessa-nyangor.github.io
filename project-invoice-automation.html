Invoice Automation

Google Apps Script invoice automation that monitors labeled Gmail messages, processes PDF attachments, extracts key invoice fields, records them in Google Sheets, and logs exceptions.

Automation / Workflow Designer Google Apps Script · Gmail · Google Sheets · Scheduled Triggers
▶ View Case Study Back to Projects

The problem

Invoice information arriving by email can require repetitive manual review and data entry before it becomes usable for tracking or reporting. The automation was designed to turn labeled invoice emails into structured records while preserving a clear error trail for items that need review.

How it works

Step 01

Watch the invoice inbox

A scheduled Apps Script checks unread Gmail messages carrying the invoice label so the workflow starts from a controlled intake queue.

Step 02

Process PDF attachments

The script reviews attachments, skips non-PDF files, and sends each invoice PDF through the extraction routine.

Step 03

Extract and structure key fields

Parsing rules capture vendor, total amount, invoice number, invoice date, tax, category, received date, filename, and a confidence score when the fields can be identified.

Step 04

Write, log, and monitor

Successful records are appended to Google Sheets, processed messages are marked read, and errors are written to a dedicated log with notification and system-health checks for follow-up.

Design approach

  • Use a Gmail label and unread state to keep the automation focused on a defined invoice-processing queue.
  • Separate essential fields from optional fields and calculate a confidence score so incomplete extraction is easier to identify.
  • Centralize successful records and error history in Google Sheets instead of leaving processing results scattered across email.
  • Include scheduled triggers, health checks, logging, and error notifications so the workflow can be monitored rather than operating as a black box.

Outcome

A working Google Apps Script automation pattern that turns invoice emails into structured spreadsheet records and reduces repetitive invoice transcription while keeping exceptions visible for manual review.

What this demonstrates

Google Apps Script, Gmail and Google Sheets automation, scheduled triggers, PDF-oriented data extraction, pattern matching, validation, confidence scoring, error handling, operational logging, and workflow design.
