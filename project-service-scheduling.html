Ontario Service Scheduling System

Constraint-based service scheduling built around technician capability, geography, client windows, dependencies, and urgent work.

Operations / Scheduling Designer Zoho · Google Sheets · Apps Script · Scheduling Logic
▶ View Case Study Back to Projects

The problem

Field-service scheduling is not simply a calendar problem. Each assignment can depend on technician capability, travel, client availability, shutdown windows, parts, on-call coverage, vacations, multi-technician requirements, and the priority of emergency work.

How it works

Step 01

Collect the work

Service contracts, service requests, client commitments, technician availability, and dependency notes are consolidated into a working Ontario schedule.

Step 02

Apply operating constraints

Each job is checked against technician capability, duration, geography, travel, staffing requirements, parts readiness, client windows, on-call coverage, time off, and fixed commitments.

Step 03

Build and audit assignments

Jobs are grouped geographically where practical, heavier work is protected from overbooking, and the schedule is reviewed for conflicts, gaps, duplicate work, unresolved dependencies, and route inefficiencies.

Step 04

Carry changes through completion

Reschedules and urgent work are reflected in client communication, technician assignments, completion documentation, reporting, and the downstream service-to-invoice handoff.

Design approach

  • Treat geography, technician capability, and service duration as core scheduling dimensions rather than assuming every open time slot is interchangeable.
  • Separate fixed commitments from moveable work so urgent changes can be absorbed without rebuilding the entire month.
  • Maintain operational and per-technician views so workload, on-call capacity, days off, and coverage gaps remain visible.
  • Use structured audits and source-of-truth exports to catch missing bookings, duplicates, overdue work, and unresolved dependencies.

Outcome

A repeatable scheduling approach for complex Ontario field-service work that makes operational constraints visible and connects calendar decisions to client communication, technician execution, reporting, and follow-through.

What this demonstrates

Constraint-based scheduling, operations coordination, resource allocation, exception management, CRM discipline, and spreadsheet-supported workflow design.
